## Furnace / anvil work loops (Wren, batch crafting)

While a furnace or anvil batch runs, Wren hammers at the anvil or reaches her hands in and out of the furnace, one loop per item. These motions are not hand-keyed: `tools/work_anim/bake.js` retargets them from two **Quaternius** clips (CC0 1.0, public domain, commercial use OK) into a small table in `src/char3d.js` (chest lean plus wrist / elbow targets for her IK arms):

| Loop | Source clip | Pack | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anvil hammering | `TreeChopping_Loop` | Universal Animation Library 2 [Standard], https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary2.html | CC0 1.0 |
| Furnace reach in / out | `Interact` (the left hand; the right hand is its mirror half a loop later) | Universal Animation Library [Standard], https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html | CC0 1.0 |
