## Furnace / anvil work loops (Wren, batch crafting)

While a furnace or anvil batch runs, Wren works one loop per item. These motions are not hand-keyed: `tools/work_anim/bake.js` bakes them into a small table in `src/char3d.js` (body bend and arm angles / wrist targets for her IK limbs):

| Loop | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Anvil hammering | Theus's own forge strike (this game's `src/volcanic_forge.js`: his swing keys, abduction curve, impact solve and `strikeBody()` torso / knee motion) and his smithing hammer's shape, scaled and retargeted to her | our own work |
| Furnace: bend down, place the item in, draw it out | `Farm_PlantSeed`, Universal Animation Library 2 [Standard] by **Quaternius**, https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary2.html | CC0 1.0 |
