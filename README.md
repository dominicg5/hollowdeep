# Hollowdeep: playable prototype

One page, `index.html`, plus its `assets/` folder (painted art and the vendored Three.js). It has no CDN or server dependencies. Open it in any modern browser (Safari/Chrome on a phone, or desktop). Progress saves to the browser's localStorage.

**Test mode is ON by default:** 1 game hour = 1 real minute. Training thresholds are also scaled ×0.01 (+100% boost at 3,000 taps, daily cap 600). Toggle it, skip time, or reset in ⚙️ Settings.

## How to play
- **🗺️ Ledgeville** (opens first): a 3D map of the world around the Hollow, drawn with Three.js (r149, vendored in `assets/vendor/three.min.js`, no CDN). This is the approved base only; more gets added on top of it later.
  - **Terrain**: the Hollow is a giant, empty pit (radius 3,000, about 55% of the plateau's width, no title over it) on the flat summit of a towering plateau (radius about 5,400, summit height `CITY.summitH` = 1,800 over the lowlands, world radius 13,000). Its walls are natural: broken, irregular rock-strata ledges of varied depth (not concentric rings), undercut bands, patchy moss and roots dangling below the lip. The plateau edge is natural too: an irregular, domain-warped outline, tall layered cliff bands and sheer faces on several sides, craggy peaks and shoulders on the rim, rocky buttresses and spurs, and foothill ramps elsewhere. Haze pools in the lowlands below. Rock shows on steep slopes and grass on gentle ones. Fissures carve through the ridges, and where they meet the Hollow they keep cutting as channels through the lip and down the pit wall. Fissures crack outward across rolling green lowlands, and snowy, rocky mountain ranges ring the edge. There is flat grass and a few trees. There is **no water** for now: no lake, sea, rivers, waterfalls, boats or docks.
  - **Homes are Ledges**: each one is displayed as "*Name*'s Ledge" (like a portaledge) and uses the concept-art tiers (Rimside Hut, Lantern Croft, Ropewalk Cottage, Tetherwright House, Ladderstack, Spinehouse, Lumenworks, Deepwarden Hall). The count comes from one config value, `CITY.homeCount` (760 for now). Homes and trees are real 3D models (four house models mapped onto the eight tiers, with scale, rotation and tint variation; oaks in the lowlands, sequoias on mountain slopes and in the snow, willows along the cliffs and the rim). Each home has a little life around it: fenced yards, crates, barrels, a lantern post (dim at night), chimney smoke on some, and a few dim window lights at night. When zoomed far out, homes switch to cheap roof-colour dots.
  - **🌳 The Heartwood**: one giant willow on the Hollow's lip, leaning out over the pit, its roots gripping the lip and the wall. The top 1% of players (`CITY.heartwoodShare`; 8 at 760) live in it: small platforms and portaledges with tiny homes on the canopy and hung over the Hollow, linked by rope walkways and ladders, with dim lanterns. From a great branch a pulley (beam, wheel, rope and a winch on the lip) lowers a crate into the Hollow, swaying and slowly rising and falling.
  - **Rings by rank.** Every player is ranked by deepest Hollow level reached (99 numbered levels; 99 is a goalpost), then split into deciles: the top 1% live in the Heartwood, ring 1 (the rest of the top 10%) sits on the summit by the lip, and each following 10% forms the next ring outward, down the foothills and across the lowlands, until ring 10 (bottom 10%) reaches the mountain slopes at the edge of the map (`CITY.rings`). Within a ring homes gather in small clusters with natural jitter, follow the terrain, and avoid cliffs and crevices. AI depths are mostly shallow with a few deep (median about Lv 6, nothing near 99). Your own Ledge is placed by your rank and moves rings, into the Heartwood too, as it changes. For now, each Hollow layer your hero has reached counts as 6 levels. Labels and cards show **Deepest: Lv N** and the ring (or Heartwood).
  - **🏆 Rim Leaderboard** (HUD button): ranks every Ledge by rim proximity (deepest level, then distance to the rim) with rank, name, deepest level and ring (🌳 marks Heartwood residents). Your row is highlighted and pinned on top. Proximity perks are a placeholder: "(coming soon)".
  - **⚖️ The Exchange**: one grand market hall near the Hollow. It is a free player market where resources sell for Lumen at player-set prices. Tap it to see an order book of AI listings (seller, quantity, asking price in Lumen). You can buy with your Lumen, or post your own sell listing at your own price; the goods are held until you cancel. Buyers for player listings are coming soon.
  - **The surface is safe**: no raiding from the map. A home card shows the owner, tier, deepest level, rim rank and ring, "Perks: (coming soon)" and a Visit placeholder.
  - **Day/night**: house lanterns are dim and sparse, and at night the Hollow's pulsing gold-to-teal Lumen glow is the dominant light (tone-mapped, no white blowout), lighting the pit walls and the nearest roofs. Gentle clouds, sun shadows and haze.
  - **Camera**: one finger drags to pan, pinch zooms, a two-finger twist rotates, and a two-finger vertical drag tilts (clamped 35 to 70 degrees). On desktop: the wheel zooms and right-drag or ctrl-drag rotates and tilts. HUD buttons: ＋/－, 🏠 (fly home), 🌐 (whole map), 🏆 (leaderboard), ⟲/⟳ (rotate 45 degrees), ⛰️ (tilt). Labels stay pinned to the world, and the minimap shows the view footprint.
  - Removed from the map for now: the Rim Mole and all random or world events, festivals and seasons, the rumor ticker, named townsfolk, pets on the map, landmarks, lift towers, cranes, scaffolds, set-piece outposts and the Rift Pass, bridges, stairs, the journal and legend, and all raiding from the map.
- **🏰 Capital**: one builder at a time. Upgrade the Lantern Hall (income), Storehouse (caps and raid protection), Training Grounds, Tether Lift (how deep your Spine reaches, lift capacity, and a cheaper Pull), Guardian Wall (rock golems; from Lv 6 it also needs Obsidian), Kennel (pet slots), and Rim Forge (smelts raw ore at the top). Obsidian comes from the Bellows layer, raids on Emberwound homes, or the Cinderglass Bazaar.
- **🕳️ Hollow**: send Wren on timed expeditions. The Lip takes 1h, Terraces 3h, Rootfall 6h, and the Bellows 10h. Deeper trips pay better but are riskier. A failed run leaves her "stranded" (half loot, home later, or pay Lumen for a rescue line). Expeditions can also find pet eggs.
- **🐢 Outposts**: your Strider *Longstep* mines a vein and lifts its goods to the Rim every hour. Hauling costs Lumen (**the Pull** = weight × depth × layer pull). Install a **Smelter** (iron + coal → steel) or a **Lumen Still** (moss → lumen) to refine below, and set shipping to "Refined only". Moving means packing up, traveling, and settling, all on timers. It costs Crowns plus Lumen that scales with depth, and the outpost is raid bait while it's moving. At Tether Lift Lv 2 you can build a second outpost, the Tetherdrill. Outposts burn Lumen to stay lit, and they "go dark" if the capital runs out.
- **⚔️ Raids**: **off for now** (`CONFIG.raidsEnabled = false`). Nobody raids your capital or outposts (including during offline catch-up), you can't raid anyone, and the tab shows a "coming later" note. Older saves with a pending raid are cleaned up on load. Setting the flag back to `true` restores the old system (AI raids every 8 to 14 game hours, rival raids, the golem wall).
- **🗡️ Training**: tap the dummy (or hold Space). Taps feed a rolling 7-day bank: boost = 100% × (bank ÷ 300k)^0.75, with a daily cap of 60k. Taps over the cap earn cosmetic Spar Marks, and anything faster than 12 taps/sec is ignored. The boost multiplies all hero XP.
- **Offline**: close the tab and come back (more than 45s later). The game simulates the gap (up to 72 game hours) and shows a "While you were away" summary.

## What to test
1. Tap the dummy 20 to 30 times. The counter, boost %, and next-milestone line should update, and the sword should swing.
2. Send Wren to The Lip and wait about 1 minute. Loot and XP should show up as a toast.
3. Raids are off for now, so there is no tutorial raid.
4. The full chain: upgrade the Tether Lift to Lv 3 (needs Steel from Rootfall/Bellows expeditions or rival raids), move Longstep to the Iron Seam (layer 4), add a Smelter, choose "Refined only", and compare the two Pull numbers on the card.
5. Close the tab for a few minutes and reopen it (or use ⚙️ → Skip +8h). Check the away summary.
6. Feel: does a 2 to 3 minute check-in feel worth it? Is moving a real decision? Is the tap boost worth tapping?

## Tuning
Every number lives in the `CONFIG` object at the top of the `<script>`: layers, loot, timers, costs, the tap curve, Pull weights, raid odds, and pets.

## Dev
- Source pieces are in `src/`: `head.html`, `game.js`, `ui.js`, and the Ledgeville map files `map_gen.js` (seeded generation), `map_draw.js` (procedural fallback painter), `art_manifest.js` + `map_art.js` (2D painter, used to paint the 3D ground textures), `map3d.js` (Three.js renderer: heightmap terrain, instanced 3D models and props, the Heartwood, lights, picking), `map_life.js` (time) and `map_ui.js` (camera, input, labels, cards, leaderboard, Exchange). Rebuild `index.html` with `./build.sh`.
- 3D models live in `assets/models/` (about 1.4 MB total): each house and tree is a meshopt-compressed binary with two LODs (near about 2.4k to 4.2k triangles, far about 300 to 600) plus 512px WebP textures, and the Heartwood uses a 14k-triangle mesh with 1024px textures. They are built from the source GLBs (kept outside the repo) by `tools/pack_models.mjs` (meshoptimizer simplify + encode, sharp for WebP). The meshopt decoder is vendored at `assets/vendor/meshopt_decoder.js`, so there is no CDN.
- Painted art lives in `assets/` (about 3.8 MB of WebP): three sprite atlases (homes in 8 tiers, mountains, lifts, landmarks, set pieces for the frontier outpost, Lake Falls Town and the Rift Pass, trees, townsfolk, pets, the Rim Mole), seamless ground, cliff and crop textures, the Hollow, and painted UI art in `assets/ui/` for the other tabs.
- Because of the image assets, serve the folder over http for local testing (for example `python3 -m http.server 8765`). `test/city.js` defaults to `http://localhost:8765/index.html`. `test/shots.js` takes quick review screenshots.
- World-generation guard: `node test/worldgen_guard.js` fails if the seeded world (mountains, crevices, sampled ground heights) changes outside the allowlisted local edits; to change the world on purpose run `UPDATE=1 node test/worldgen_guard.js` and commit `test/worldgen_guard.json` with the reason.
- Headless tests: `cd test && node test.js` (Playwright-core + system Chrome; about 80s because it waits for a real 1-hour test-mode expedition), and `node city.js` for the map (mobile viewport with SwiftShader WebGL: 3D render, drag, twist and tilt, wheel, pinch, label anchoring, home card, depth rings and relocation, Exchange, leaderboard, base-only checks, tabs, screenshots). `node g3d.js` takes 3D review screenshots from several angles, day and night.
- The map is a Three.js scene. There are two heightmap terrain meshes (the whole world, plus a finer grid over the plateau and lowlands), painted ground textures (whole world, inner world, and a 2,600-unit detail patch repainted around the camera), tiled grass and rock detail textures with anisotropic filtering, triplanar cliffs (no stretching), and snow only on high peaks. ACES tone mapping keeps the night Lumen glow (pulsing gold to teal) rich without blowing out, and the pit terraces stay visible. Homes and trees are `InstancedMesh` sets (one per model per LOD), refilled only with the instances in view, the nearest at the detailed LOD; yard props are instanced boxes and cylinders shown only near the camera, chimney smoke is a shader-animated point cloud, and the Exchange is a billboard. Homes fall back to billboards if the models fail to load. There is a soft-shadow sun (1024 shadow map on phones), a Lumen point light in the pit, fog, and DPR capped at 2. It falls back to the 2D painter if WebGL is unavailable. The world is seeded (`CITY.seed`), so every player sees the same map.

## Known limitations
- No sound. Water is off until the base is solid. Leaderboard, Exchange listings and Visit are local stubs (AI players, no server).
- Single-player: rivals, raiders and every Ledgeville Ledge are simulated AI. No server and no real PvP. Bazaar prices are local.
- Saves are per browser/device (localStorage). Clearing site data wipes them.
- The balance is a first pass and untested with real players.
- Not included yet: Gates and Wardens, Captains, line cutting/splice repair, Mother Veins, capital capture, Shellbacks, pet Moults.
