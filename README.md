# Hollowdeep: playable prototype

One page, `index.html`, plus its `assets/` folder (painted art and the vendored Three.js). It has no CDN or server dependencies. Open it in any modern browser (Safari/Chrome on a phone, or desktop). Progress saves to the browser's localStorage.

**Test mode is ON by default:** 1 game hour = 1 real minute. Training thresholds are also scaled ×0.01 (+100% boost at 3,000 taps, daily cap 600). Toggle it, skip time, or reset in ⚙️ Settings.

## How to play
- **🗺️ Rim City** (opens first): a 3D map of the world around the Hollow, drawn with Three.js (r149, vendored in `assets/vendor/three.min.js`, no CDN). This is the approved base only; more gets added on top of it later.
  - **Terrain**: the Hollow is a giant, empty, terraced pit (radius 3,000, about 55% of the plateau's width, nothing built in or over it, no title over it) on the flat summit of a raised plateau (radius about 5,400, world radius 13,000). The plateau edge is natural: an irregular, domain-warped outline with ridged-multifractal rocky spurs radiating outward, foothills rolling up to the summit, and sheer cliffs only on some stretches. Rock shows on steep slopes and grass on gentle ones, and fissures carve through the ridges. Fissures crack outward across rolling green lowlands, and snowy, rocky mountain ranges ring the edge. There is flat grass and a few trees. There is **no water** for now: no lake, sea, rivers, waterfalls, boats or docks.
  - **Homes are Ledges**: each one is displayed as "*Name*'s Ledge" (like a portaledge) and uses the concept-art tiers (Rimside Hut, Lantern Croft, Ropewalk Cottage, Tetherwright House, Ladderstack, Spinehouse, Lumenworks, Deepwarden Hall). The count comes from one config value, `CITY.homeCount` (760 for now). Most are packed in tight rings along the Hollow's lip and fewer are out in the lowlands. When zoomed far out, the rim switches to cheap roof-colour dots, so it still reads as a red-roofed ring.
  - **Rim proximity = deepest Hollow level reached.** The Hollow has 99 numbered depth levels (99 is a goalpost). Owners who have delved deepest live on the inner rings at the lip, and shallower ones live further out across the lowlands. AI depths are mostly shallow with a few deep (median about Lv 6, nothing near 99). Your own Ledge is placed by your record and moves inward when it improves. For now, each Hollow layer your hero has reached counts as 6 levels. Labels and cards show **Deepest: Lv N**.
  - **🏆 Rim Leaderboard** (HUD button): ranks every Ledge by rim proximity (deepest level, then distance to the rim) with rank, name, deepest level and ring. Your row is highlighted and pinned on top. Proximity perks are a placeholder: "(coming soon)".
  - **⚖️ The Exchange**: one grand market hall near the Hollow. It is a free player market where resources sell for Lumen at player-set prices. Tap it to see an order book of AI listings (seller, quantity, asking price in Lumen). You can buy with your Lumen, or post your own sell listing at your own price; the goods are held until you cancel. Buyers for player listings are coming soon.
  - **The surface is safe**: no raiding from the map. A home card shows the owner, tier, deepest level, rim rank and ring, "Perks: (coming soon)" and a Visit placeholder.
  - **Day/night**: house lanterns are dim and sparse, and at night the Hollow's pulsing Lumen glow is the dominant light, lighting the pit walls and the nearest roofs. Gentle clouds, sun shadows and haze.
  - **Camera**: one finger drags to pan, pinch zooms, a two-finger twist rotates, and a two-finger vertical drag tilts (clamped 35 to 70 degrees). On desktop: the wheel zooms and right-drag or ctrl-drag rotates and tilts. HUD buttons: ＋/－, 🏠 (fly home), 🌐 (whole map), 🏆 (leaderboard), ⟲/⟳ (rotate 45 degrees), ⛰️ (tilt). Labels stay pinned to the world, and the minimap shows the view footprint.
  - Removed from the map for now: the Rim Mole and all random or world events, festivals and seasons, the rumor ticker, named townsfolk, pets on the map, landmarks, lift towers, cranes, scaffolds, set-piece outposts and the Rift Pass, bridges, stairs, the journal and legend, and all raiding from the map.
- **🏰 Capital**: one builder at a time. Upgrade the Lantern Hall (income), Storehouse (caps and raid protection), Training Grounds, Tether Lift (how deep your Spine reaches, lift capacity, and a cheaper Pull), Guardian Wall (rock golems; from Lv 6 it also needs Obsidian), Kennel (pet slots), and Rim Forge (smelts raw ore at the top). Obsidian comes from the Bellows layer, raids on Emberwound homes, or the Cinderglass Bazaar.
- **🕳️ Hollow**: send Wren on timed expeditions. The Lip takes 1h, Terraces 3h, Rootfall 6h, and the Bellows 10h. Deeper trips pay better but are riskier. A failed run leaves her "stranded" (half loot, home later, or pay Lumen for a rescue line). Expeditions can also find pet eggs.
- **🐢 Outposts**: your Strider *Longstep* mines a vein and lifts its goods to the Rim every hour. Hauling costs Lumen (**the Pull** = weight × depth × layer pull). Install a **Smelter** (iron + coal → steel) or a **Lumen Still** (moss → lumen) to refine below, and set shipping to "Refined only". Moving means packing up, traveling, and settling, all on timers. It costs Crowns plus Lumen that scales with depth, and the outpost is raid bait while it's moving. At Tether Lift Lv 2 you can build a second outpost, the Tetherdrill. Outposts burn Lumen to stay lit, and they "go dark" if the capital runs out.
- **⚔️ Raids**: auto-resolved raids on 3 AI rival bases, scored by your hero + pets vs. their defense, for 0 to 3 stars of loot. AI raiders hit you every 8 to 14 game hours (the first, at 1.5h, is a tutorial raid you win). That's when the **wall stands up** and the golems wake. Raiders sometimes go after an outpost that's moving or has a full stockhold instead.
- **🗡️ Training**: tap the dummy (or hold Space). Taps feed a rolling 7-day bank: boost = 100% × (bank ÷ 300k)^0.75, with a daily cap of 60k. Taps over the cap earn cosmetic Spar Marks, and anything faster than 12 taps/sec is ignored. The boost multiplies all hero XP.
- **Offline**: close the tab and come back (more than 45s later). The game simulates the gap (up to 72 game hours) and shows a "While you were away" summary.

## What to test
1. Tap the dummy 20 to 30 times. The counter, boost %, and next-milestone line should update, and the sword should swing.
2. Send Wren to The Lip and wait about 1 minute. Loot and XP should show up as a toast and in the Chronicle (Raids tab).
3. Wait about 90 seconds from a new game for the tutorial raid and the golem reveal. You can also use Raids → "Test: trigger a raid on me".
4. The full chain: upgrade the Tether Lift to Lv 3 (needs Steel from Rootfall/Bellows expeditions or rival raids), move Longstep to the Iron Seam (layer 4), add a Smelter, choose "Refined only", and compare the two Pull numbers on the card.
5. Close the tab for a few minutes and reopen it (or use ⚙️ → Skip +8h). Check the away summary.
6. Feel: does a 2 to 3 minute check-in feel worth it? Is moving a real decision? Is the tap boost worth tapping?

## Tuning
Every number lives in the `CONFIG` object at the top of the `<script>`: layers, loot, timers, costs, the tap curve, Pull weights, raid odds, and pets.

## Dev
- Source pieces are in `src/`: `head.html`, `game.js`, `ui.js`, and the Rim City map files `map_gen.js` (seeded generation), `map_draw.js` (procedural fallback painter), `art_manifest.js` + `map_art.js` (2D painter, used to paint the 3D ground textures), `map3d.js` (Three.js renderer: heightmap terrain, billboards, lights, picking), `map_life.js` (time) and `map_ui.js` (camera, input, labels, cards, leaderboard, Exchange). Rebuild `index.html` with `./build.sh`.
- Painted art lives in `assets/` (about 3.8 MB of WebP): three sprite atlases (homes in 8 tiers, mountains, lifts, landmarks, set pieces for the frontier outpost, Lake Falls Town and the Rift Pass, trees, townsfolk, pets, the Rim Mole), seamless ground, cliff and crop textures, the Hollow, and painted UI art in `assets/ui/` for the other tabs.
- Because of the image assets, serve the folder over http for local testing (for example `python3 -m http.server 8765`). `test/city.js` defaults to `http://localhost:8765/index.html`. `test/shots.js` takes quick review screenshots.
- Headless tests: `cd test && node test.js` (Playwright-core + system Chrome; about 100s because it waits for a real 1-hour test-mode expedition and the tutorial raid), and `node city.js` for the map (mobile viewport with SwiftShader WebGL: 3D render, drag, twist and tilt, wheel, pinch, label anchoring, home card, depth rings and relocation, Exchange, leaderboard, base-only checks, tabs, screenshots). `node g3d.js` takes 3D review screenshots from several angles, day and night.
- The map is a Three.js scene. There are two heightmap terrain meshes (the whole world, plus a finer grid over the plateau and lowlands), painted ground textures (whole world, inner world, and a 2,600-unit detail patch repainted around the camera), tiled grass and rock detail textures with anisotropic filtering, triplanar cliffs (no stretching), and snow only on high peaks. ACES tone mapping keeps the night Lumen glow (pulsing gold to teal) rich without blowing out, and the pit terraces stay visible. Homes, trees and the Exchange are instanced billboards. There is a soft-shadow sun (1024 shadow map on phones), a Lumen point light in the pit, fog, and DPR capped at 2. It falls back to the 2D painter if WebGL is unavailable. The world is seeded (`CITY.seed`), so every player sees the same map.

## Known limitations
- No sound. Homes are camera-facing billboards (no low-poly close-ups yet). Water is off until the base is solid. Leaderboard, Exchange listings and Visit are local stubs (AI players, no server).
- Single-player: rivals, raiders and every Rim City Ledge are simulated AI. No server and no real PvP. Bazaar prices are local.
- Saves are per browser/device (localStorage). Clearing site data wipes them.
- The balance is a first pass and untested with real players.
- Not included yet: Gates and Wardens, Captains, line cutting/splice repair, Mother Veins, capital capture, Shellbacks, pet Moults.
